# Iridescent

**Native feel, next level function.** A podcast player for iPhone.

Every episode transcribed on your phone, ads skipped, chapters where there were none, and your free and members' feeds
as one show — built with Apple's own controls and Liquid Glass. No account, no tracking.

- **Get it:** iPhone and iPad — [join the TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/zXPsmnuT) · App Store —
  coming soon · Mac — a download here when it's ready
- **Privacy:** [Privacy Policy](https://iridescentpodcasts.com/privacy.html)
- **Support:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Alkarim-Billawala/iridescent-app/issues/new/choose) — issues are public,
  so please leave out anything personal

This is Iridescent's public home: its website, its feature list and its issue tracker. Iridescent isn't open source — the
app's code isn't here.

## Features

The highlights. See the **[Full Feature List](FEATURES.md)** for everything.

### Every episode, transcribed on your iPhone
Read along as it plays. Tap a line to jump there. Search every word in your library. An hour of audio takes under a
minute, right on your phone — and if a show publishes its own transcript, it's there at once. The episode you're playing
always comes first.

### Ads skipped. You just keep listening.¹
Ads, intros, credits and fundraising appeals, found and skipped as you listen. You land on the show's first word, not the
end of a jingle. Undo is one tap, and you decide what gets skipped — everywhere, or show by show.

### Chapters, even when the show has none¹
Iridescent writes chapters from the transcript, where the conversation actually moves on, with the ad breaks shown between
them. Shows with their own chapters keep theirs. Slide a thumb down the transcript's edge to jump to any chapter. Chapters
you've heard are marked as heard.

### Your chapters, your order
Drag an episode's chapters into the order you want, and it plays that way — the scrubber, the transcript, the times and the
skip buttons all follow. Want the interview first and the news last? Done. Untick a chapter and it stays skipped.

### Who's talking
Every voice told apart, right in the transcript — each speaker's name at their turn, a thin colour beside their lines, and
how much each one talked. Iridescent names the hosts and guests for you,¹ or you name them once and it recognises them by
voice in every episode, in every show.

### One show, every feed
Pay for an ad-free or members' feed? Iridescent pairs it with the free one: one show in your library, your history from
both. The members' bonus parts get their own chapters, and the free copy's ads are found just by comparing the two — no AI
key needed. Your ad-free copy stays ad-free.

### Switch copies without missing a beat
Switch copies and you land on the same sentence — a moment early, never past it. Already heard the free episode? Its
members' copy plays only the bonus you haven't heard.

### Local first. AI last.
Transcripts, the voice and silence processing, summaries, who's speaking, the music, the free copy's ads — all made on
your phone, privately, at no cost. Only what the phone can't do yet goes out¹ — finding ads and writing chapters in a new
episode, fixing misheard names — in one request per episode, under your own key.

### Search. Everything.
One field finds anything you have — a show, an episode, a chapter, or the moment someone said it, in any transcript in
your library — as you type, with no connection needed. Tap a line and it plays from there.

### Clearer voices, less waiting
Speech Enhancer evens out loud and quiet voices. Trim Silence shortens pauses without touching a word. Speed up to 3× and
voices still sound natural.

### On your Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center
Widgets for what's playing and what's next, Control Center buttons, and quick actions on the app icon.

### Rewind
Your listening, live — not saved for the end of the year. Time listened, time saved, top shows, streaks. And History:
everything you've heard and changed, each with Undo.

¹ *Uses your own Claude API key — optional. Everything else works without one.*

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it need an account?</strong></summary>

No. There's nothing to sign up for.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I bring my library?</strong></summary>

Yes — import an OPML file from your current app, played episodes included.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Do I need an AI key?</strong></summary>

No. Transcripts, search, who's talking, summaries and the rest work on your phone without one. Add your own Claude API
key if you want ads and chapters found in every episode; you can see what it costs and set spending limits in the app.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What does it collect?</strong></summary>

Nothing. Your library, transcripts and listening history stay on your device. The
[Privacy Policy](https://iridescentpodcasts.com/privacy.html) lists everything your device sends, and
to whom.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is it open source?</strong></summary>

No. This repository holds the website, the feature list and the issue tracker; the app's code isn't published.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I report a problem or ask a question?</strong></summary>

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Alkarim-Billawala/iridescent-app/issues/new/choose) — the show, the episode and what
happened is usually all it takes. Issues are public, so please leave out anything personal, and never paste a private
feed's link.
</details>
