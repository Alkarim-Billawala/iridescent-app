# Iridescent — Every Feature

Everything Iridescent does today, area by area. Lines marked ¹ use your own Claude API key — optional; everything else
works without one, on your iPhone.

## Playing

- Plays any podcast through Iridescent's own audio engine, streaming and downloading at once: no waiting for a download,
  and whatever it fetched stays on the phone.
- Seeks anywhere quickly, even into a part not downloaded yet.
- Copes with flaky networks: a dropped connection retries on its own and picks up where it was.
- Knows when a show's server swaps the file (new inserted ads) and starts the new copy at the same time, without a tap.
- **The next episode starts instantly**: while one plays, the opening of what's next is fetched ahead — on cellular only
  near the end, and only if you allow it.
- **Speed** from 0.5× to 3× in small steps, with quick presets; per podcast or for everything.
- **Speed Algorithm**: Natural (voices stay natural up to 2×), Spectral or Smooth.
- **Speech Enhancer**: Balanced evens out loud and quiet voices and quiet shows; Warm is a gentler, fixed tone; or Off.
- **Trim Silence** shortens pauses without touching a word, with soft joins so background music carries across.
- The live speed under the speed button — what you're really hearing, after trimming — and the episode's average.
- Mono shows play at full volume on both sides.
- AirPlay and HomePod: the transcript, chapters and skips stay in step with what you hear, not what's buffered — and
  **AirPlay Transcript Sync** tunes the timing to your own speakers, live while you listen.
- Calls and other apps interrupt politely: playback resumes only if it was playing.
- **Sleep timer**: minutes (adjustable as it runs), end of chapter, or end of episode.
- Resumes a couple of seconds before where you left off, for context.
- Marks an episode played when you reach its credits (or near the end) while it keeps playing.
- **Lock screen and Control Center**: artwork (the chapter's picture when it has one), skip buttons as you set them, the
  scrubber; headphone and car buttons.
- **Skip buttons your way**: 5–60 seconds, Chapter Start / Next Chapter, Episode Start / Next Episode — in the app and on
  the lock screen.
- **Quick taps jump chapters**: tap skip a few times quickly and the next goes to the next chapter; a skip never
  overshoots a chapter start.
- **Back to where you were**: any jump of more than a minute offers "Back to 12:34 · Undo", several steps deep.

## Transcripts

- Every episode transcribed **on your iPhone** — an hour of audio in under a minute with the app open, privately, free.
- **The episode you're playing always comes first**: anything else being transcribed steps aside and picks up later.
- A show's own published transcript shows at once, until Iridescent's is ready.
- **Read along**: three lines under the artwork grow into the full transcript; the spoken line in bold, followed as it
  plays.
- **Tap any line** to play from there. Scroll away and **Follow** waits in the corner to bring you back.
- **Drag the scrubber and the transcript follows your finger**, line by line.
- Chapter headings in the transcript; ads and other skips folded into one line ("Ad · 2:08") that opens with a tap.
- **The chapter rail**: slide a thumb down the transcript's edge to see every chapter and jump to one.
- **Find in the transcript**: matches highlighted and counted, dots on the rail, a find bar that rides your keyboard.
- Web addresses read aloud are written as addresses ("cbc.ca/podcasts").
- Misheard names and words corrected, without inventing names.¹
- **Music in the transcript**: a "♪ Music · 0:24" row where music plays on its own, and a quiet mark down lines spoken
  over it.
- **Work ahead**: your top shows' new episodes are transcribed ahead of time — overnight on the charger, and early in the
  morning for overnight releases — so they're ready when you press play. Per show: Auto (your top shows), Always or Never;
  a playlist can ask for Always.
- **Transcribe Now** from any episode's menu — and **Stop Transcribing** while it's being made.
- **Background Runs**: what was done in the background, when, and how each went, in plain words.
- A second copy of an episode (free vs members') isn't transcribed from scratch — only the parts that differ. Nor is a
  fresh copy a show's server sends with new ads.
- Background work waits when the phone runs warm; never does the same work twice.

## Who's talking

- **Every voice told apart**, on your iPhone: each speaker's name above their turn and a thin colour bar down their lines;
  quick back-and-forths kept together.
- **Names**: the hosts and guests named from the episode,¹ spelled the way the show spells them; ad voices are never named.
- **Name a speaker yourself** — once — and they're recognised by voice in every episode, in every show, with no AI call.
  Say two voices are the same person, or that one isn't a person at all. Every change has Undo.
- **People**: everyone you've named, the shows they're in; rename, merge or forget anyone.
- **The speaker legend**: each person's share of the episode, and Stats — time, words and words per minute — with ads left
  out. Voices under a minute fold into "Other Voices".
- **Speaker Colours**: Match the Show, Same Everywhere, Distinct or Main Speaker. Show Speakers per podcast; Speaker
  Detection Auto, Always or Never, per podcast or playlist.

## Ads, intros, credits and appeals

- **Ads, intros, credits and fundraising appeals found and skipped** as you listen.¹
- You land on the show's first word, not the end of a jingle — skips are snapped to real pauses.
- Choose what's skipped — Ads, Sponsor Chapters, Appeals, Intros, Credits — everywhere or show by show.
- **Undo** on every skip ("Skipped ad · Undo"): it plays what was skipped and won't skip it again.
- Tap a skipped row in the chapter list to hear it this once.
- Shows that mark their own sponsor chapters are skipped from those, with no AI at all.
- **The free copy's ads found for free**: with a paired members' feed, ads are found just by comparing the two copies, and
  named from what the read says — no AI key needed.
- **Your ad-free copy stays ad-free**: nothing in a members' copy is skipped as an ad.
- **Music found on your iPhone** in every transcribed episode — Auto, Always or Never, per show.
- Time saved by skipping counts in Rewind, by kind.

## Chapters

- **Chapters even when the show has none**, written from the transcript where the conversation moves on.¹
- The show's own chapters kept when it has them — from the file, the feed or the show notes; times in show notes placed
  where they really start in your copy.¹
- Ad breaks shown between chapters; a chapter interrupted by a break continues after it.
- Chapter pictures in the player and on the lock screen.
- **Untick a chapter to skip it** in this episode; **Skip in Every Episode** for a recurring segment.
- **Your own chapter order**: drag chapters into the order you want and it plays that way — scrubber, transcript, times,
  skip buttons and sleep timer all follow; an unstarted episode starts with the chapter you put first. Reset or Undo any
  time.
- **Your choices on every copy**: untick, re-tick or reorder chapters on one copy of a paired episode and the others
  follow; one Undo takes them back.
- The time left in a chapter leaves out what will be skipped.
- **Chapters you've heard are marked as heard.**
- **Chapter Sources**: switch between what plays, the show's own as published and Iridescent's own — where they differ
  (a setting, off by default).

## Your library

- Follow any show by search, link, an Apple Podcasts link or the Directory; bring your whole library over from an OPML
  file, played episodes included.
- **Members' and private feeds**: a login is asked for and kept in your Keychain; every private feed wears a crown.
- **Episode limits**: keep just the latest, the latest few, the current season or everything unplayed — serial shows stay
  in order and follow your place. Per show, with defaults.
- **Hide Trailers and Bonuses** (on by default); **Show Played Episodes** in playlists and on show pages, your way.
- Shows that have gone quiet, or whose feed broke, step aside to their own row.
- **Save an episode** without following its show.
- **Mark a whole show played** in one go — new episodes still come in unplayed, and Undo puts every episode back.
- **In Progress**: every started episode, wherever it came from.
- **New Episode Dot**: a small dot on the shows you actually listen to that have something new.
- **NEW** on new episodes — seen once anywhere, gone everywhere.
- A show's page has its **whole back catalogue**, newest or oldest first. Titles sort past The / A / An.
- Library layouts: List, Compact, Detailed, Grid, Large Grid; sort and filter.
- **Share** an episode or a show — always a public link, never a members' feed.
- **Sharp artwork**, even when a show's feed only offers a tiny image; an episode's own artwork always comes first.

## Playlists and what plays next

- **Up Next** that does what you expect: queue by hand (Play Next / Play Last), then the playlist or list you played from.
- A new episode in the playlist you're playing plays next — or keep your place, per playlist.
- **Playlists** with their own symbol and colour, shows, sort, episode limit, length and date filters; pin them to Home.
- A playlist set to Always gets its episodes transcribed, its speakers found and summaries made ahead of time.
- When a playlist ends: stop, or carry on into Recently Released.
- **Swipe**: right to Play Next or Play Last; left to mark played (with Undo), download, or more.
- One menu, in one order, on every episode wherever you long-press it or tap its "…".

## Home, widgets and quick actions

- **Up Next cards**: big, coloured from the artwork, with a summary, a play button showing how far in you are and the time
  left, and the episode's menu.
- Shelves for In Progress, Saved, Playlists and each pinned playlist; **Recently Released** with NEW since your last visit;
  Recently Played and Recently Added if you want them. Arrange Home your way.
- **The play button on every row**: how far in you are, the time left, a tap to pause.
- **Widgets**: Now Playing (Home Screen and Lock Screen) with skip and play buttons that work right there, and Up Next —
  each row plays with a tap.
- **Control Center**: Play or Pause, Skip Back, Skip Forward, and Play Playlist for any playlist you pick.
- **Quick actions** on the app icon: Resume Episode, Play Most Recent Playlist, Search, Check for New Episodes.

## Paired feeds — one show, every feed

- Pay for an ad-free or members' feed? Iridescent finds its free twin and makes them **one show**: one row, one page, one
  set of settings, your history from both. You're asked when it isn't sure.
- **Switch copies without missing a beat**: land on the same sentence — a moment early, never past it — with a note saying
  where.
- The members' copy arriving while you're on the free one swaps in at the next pause; if a members' feed stops working,
  the free copy takes over.
- The members' bonus parts become their own chapters, crowned.
- A live, unedited recording gets the edited episode's chapters, with the extra parts marked.
- A specials feed folds into its show; any feed can be hidden but stay paired; name each copy your way.
- The second copy gets its transcript, chapters and ads from the first, with no new AI request.
- **Bonus Backlog**: already played the free episode? Its members' copy plays only the parts you haven't heard.
- **Play Bonus**: an episode with members' bonus you haven't heard says so — "♔ Bonus Content · 12 min" — and one tap
  plays it, then carries on from where you were.
- Unfollow one feed or all of them; Unpair any time — nothing is lost, and Undo puts it back.

## Search — Search. Everything.

- **One field over your whole library** — shows, episodes, chapters and every transcript line — as you type, on your
  phone, no connection needed; tap a line and it plays from that moment.
- **Chapters in every episode, downloaded or not**, read ahead on Wi-Fi.
- **Search one show**: its episodes, chapters and transcripts only, and Search Next 100 Episodes for more.
- **Find** in any playlist, Recently Released, In Progress and Saved; in a transcript; in your Library and every show
  picker; in History; and in Settings — type a setting's name and switch it right there.
- **The Directory**: search, Top Charts, categories, publisher pages, and **You May Like** from what you actually listen
  to. Browse, play and save from a show before following it.

## Summaries

- One or two sentences on what an episode is about, made **on your iPhone** from its show notes — or written from the
  whole episode.¹
- On every list's preview line, Up Next cards and the episode page.

## The player and the Episode Page

- The player opens out of the mini player and folds back into it, coloured from the artwork.
- Show Notes, Transcript, Chapters and Up Next slide over the artwork.
- The scrubber opens into chapters with skips striped while you drag; it never jumps to where you touched.
- Speed, sleep and sound settings grow out of the speed button; **Customize for This Podcast**.
- **The Episode Page** for any episode: notes with working times and links, its chapters, its transcript, and its actions.

## Settings

- A few short groups, like your iPhone's; the same groups for each podcast and each playlist.
- **Find any setting by typing its name.**
- Any setting for everything, or for one podcast; each setting shows which podcasts have their own.
- Every setting change is in History with Undo; **Reset All to Defaults** when you want a clean start.

## Your AI key and what it costs¹

- Add your own Claude API key for ads, chapters, name fixes and speaker names; without one, those switches are simply
  off and everything else works.
- **Usage**: what each call cost — today, this month and in all, by show.
- **Spend Limits**: the most to spend in a day, week, month or in all; when one is reached, detection waits until it
  resets.

## Rewind and History

- **Rewind**, live all year: time listened, time saved (speed, trimming, skips), top shows, streaks, when you listen most.
- **History**: every sitting and every change, by day, each with **Undo / Redo** — even "undo that listening". Filter it
  by show or kind, and search it.

## Storage, battery and privacy

- No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. [Privacy Policy](https://iridescentpodcasts.com/privacy.html)
- **Downloads ahead of time** for the shows you listen to and the playlists you play from, finishing after you leave the
  app; Download, Stream or Auto for everything, a podcast or a playlist; Wi-Fi only unless you allow cellular.
- **Audio you won't play clears itself away**, on timings you choose; transcripts and chapters stay. **Do Not Offload**
  keeps any episode.
- **New episodes found in the background**, about hourly, reading only what changed.
- Light on battery: work ahead only on the charger, nothing running between jobs, background work held back when warm.

## Notifications

- **Episode Notifications** for the shows you listen to (the default), every show, or none; On or Off for any podcast or
  playlist. Play, Play Next or Download from the notification.
- **Notification Hours**: always, only while the sun is up where you are, or your own hours.

## Shortcuts, Siri and the Action button

- More than 100 actions, named in the app's own words: play, queue, speed, sleep, skipping, chapters, speakers, playlists,
  downloads, notifications, paired feeds, Undo and more. Episodes, playlists, podcasts and people as parameters.
